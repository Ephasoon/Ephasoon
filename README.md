About
I'm Ephraim Shibru, a developer from Ethiopia interested in building useful digital products at the intersection of software engineering and business. My work and learning span web applications, management systems, backend services, databases, and AI-powered tools.

I care about understanding the problem behind the software, building maintainable solutions, and creating technology that helps people and organizations work better.
What I Do
- Build web applications, APIs, and business management systems.
- Develop and explore healthcare, education, and hospitality software.
- Experiment with AI gateways, automation, and AI-powered applications.

- Keep learning about backend architecture, databases, deployment, and product development.
